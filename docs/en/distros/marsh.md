# Marsh

**Desktop:** KDE Plasma 6.8 · **Base:** Fedora 45 · **Architecture:** x86_64

Marsh is the flagship edition of CapivaraOS. KDE Plasma is one of the most customisable desktops on Linux, and Marsh arrives with it already arranged: global menu bar at the top, dock at the bottom, dark theme, and the project's visual identity from boot to desktop.

It is the most feature-rich of the three, and the one that asks the most of the machine.

## What ships with it

- **KDE Plasma 6.8** on a Wayland session
- **Firefox** as the default browser
- **LibreOffice**, with Portuguese translation
- **VLC** for video and audio
- **KeePassXC** for passwords
- **Snapper**, for Btrfs snapshots
- **COS Dev Center** and **COS Config Center**, exclusive to CapivaraOS

## For developers

[COS Dev Center](../apps/cos-dev.md) opens at first boot and sets up the workstation: local AI running on your own machine, per-user language tracks, dev tools and GitHub configured — no terminal.

## Visual identity

Theme, icons, Plymouth and GRUB coherent from boot to desktop, with a wallpaper collection of our own. On Marsh the **wallpaper changes by itself with the time of day**.

!!! tip "Switching between light and dark"
    Switch through **Colors**, in System Settings, not through the Global Theme — Breeze's stock Global Theme overrides our settings and undoes part of the identity.

## Requirements

See [Hardware requirements](../installation/requirements.md). Marsh is the heaviest of the three; on more modest machines, [Pup](pup.md) delivers the same toolkit with a lighter desktop.

## Upgrading

From Series 2 on, Marsh upgrades **without reinstalling**, from one version to the next and from one series to the following. See [Updating the system](../update/index.md).
